About Us

Position Xero was built on one frustration: watching good businesses get burned by agencies that optimized for their own margins instead of client results.

Our Story

Why Position Xero Exists

We started Position Xero after seeing the same story play out over and over: a business owner invests $1,500 a month in Google Ads, gets a pretty report full of impressions and clicks, and still has no leads to show for it. They switch agencies. Same result. They give up on digital marketing entirely.

The problem isn't the platform. Google Ads and Meta Ads work. SEO works. Lead generation works. The problem is agencies that don't treat your money like their own, that hide behind vanity metrics and never get to the actual goal: qualified leads that turn into revenue.

Position Xero was built to be different. We measure one thing: cost per qualified lead. Everything else, impressions, clicks, reach, engagement, is secondary. We report on what actually matters to your business, and we don't stop optimizing until the numbers make sense.

We're one of the few agencies in the US that offers LLM SEO as a core service, optimizing your brand to be recommended by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini alongside traditional Google ranking. Because the future of search is already here, and most of your competitors haven't noticed yet.
