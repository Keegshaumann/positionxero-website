FAQ

Ads Questions Answered

For most US service businesses, a minimum of $500 per month in ad spend is recommended for Google Ads to generate meaningful data and leads. Combined with management from $850/month, your total investment should be based on your target CPL multiplied by the number of leads you need per month. For a deeper breakdown, see our guide on how much Google Ads cost.

A good ROAS for Google Ads is typically 3x to 5x for e-commerce. For service businesses, ROAS is less useful, we focus on cost per lead (CPL) and cost per acquisition (CPA) instead. A good CPL depends on your average deal value: if your average client is worth $7,500, a CPL of $1,000 is excellent.

Neither is universally better. Google Ads targets high-intent buyers actively searching for your service, ideal for service businesses. Meta Ads target by interests and behavior, ideal for awareness, retargeting, and visual products. Most businesses benefit from running both: Google Ads for leads, Meta Ads for brand and warm retargeting. Our full comparison of Google Ads vs Meta Ads walks through when to use each.

Google Ads can start generating leads within the first week of launch, though full optimization takes 4 to 8 weeks. Meta Ads typically take 1 to 2 weeks to exit the learning phase before performance stabilises. Budget for a 60 to 90 day ramp-up period when evaluating any new campaign.
