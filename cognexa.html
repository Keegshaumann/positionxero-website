Why It Matters

Search Is Splitting In Two

People still type queries into Google. They also ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini, and those answers cite a handful of sources instead of listing ten links. Being one of the cited sources is a different job from ranking first.

Ranking is not citing

A page can rank on page one and still never be quoted in an AI answer. The retrieval step rewards clear, self-contained, well-structured passages, not just backlinks.

Zero-click is growing

When the answer appears in the results, fewer people click through. Being the brand named inside that answer is worth more than a link nobody follows.

It compounds quietly

Models are retrained and re-crawled continuously. The work you do now shapes which brand gets named when someone asks the buying question a year from now.
