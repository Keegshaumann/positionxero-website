# Position Xero — Competitor & Workflow Analysis (2026-07-11)

> **Method:** 52-agent research run — 16 competitors profiled from their own live pages (pricing, process, services, about) plus review-signal searches; the 2 most load-bearing claims per profile independently re-fetched and verified (**26/32 checks passed** — failed checks are treated as unreliable below); two strategy analyses (operations + positioning) filtered everything for "implementable by two founders." Facts below cite what was actually fetched on 2026-07-11; competitor sites change — re-verify before quoting externally.

## The headline finding

**11 of 16 competitors hide their pricing behind a sales call.** The few who publish it are either 3–6× our price floors (Hook), carry a lock-in (LYFE's mandatory 3-month initial term), or are SaaS-for-realtors (Avenue). The dominant delivery model — an account manager fronting a specialist pod — produces the field's most consistent verified complaints (AM turnover, post-onboarding communication drop-off). **Nobody in the set occupies our intersection: published pricing + true month-to-month + founder-direct + 24h SLA + accessible floors.** Hook explicitly refuses businesses under $3M revenue — that abandoned segment is our pond.

## Where we already win (don't touch these)

1. **Founder-direct** — the field's #1 verified complaint is AM turnover/handoff drop-off (Disruptive, Thrive, KlientBoost, Hook's "average of five experts monthly"). A two-founder shop *cannot* reassign your account manager. Say it in those words.
2. **Published pricing at accessible floors** — only 3/16 publish anything; none at our price point without a lock-in.
3. **True month-to-month** — vs LYFE's 3-month minimum and Scorpion's verified lose-your-site-on-exit model. Ready-made contrast copy.
4. **24h response SLA** — literally no competitor publishes any response-time commitment. Move it into proposals/contracts to make it load-bearing.
5. **Open-book CPL reporting** — nobody markets it; verified complaints hit thin reporting at Disruptive and Thrive. Needs operationalizing (see Adopt #3/4) to stay a win.
6. **Ungated ROI calculator** — most competitors gate value behind audit forms; only KlientBoost compares.
7. **AI SEO at $600–$1,000/mo entry** — contested space (Hook charges $4,000/mo for AEO) but we're the cheapest credible entry, and we can *demonstrate* it (see Adopt #10).

## Adopt (prioritized, all two-founder-feasible)

**This week, ~zero cost:**
1. **"You own everything" written guarantee** (from Rankings.io; anti-Scorpion) — one site paragraph + contract clause: ad accounts in your name, site on your hosting, leave anytime with everything. Converts the field's most toxic verified complaint into our sales copy. *(Claude can ship the site copy on request.)*
2. **Create Clutch + Google Business-adjacent review profiles now** — the verified-review field in our verticals is nearly EMPTY (Blue Corona 17 yrs/0 Clutch reviews; RYNO 0; Staffing Scale 0; Contractor Dynamics 0). 5 verified Clutch reviews in year one puts us ahead of 17-year incumbents. Bake the ask into month-3 reporting.
3. **Launch-speed promises in writing** (Falcon/KlientBoost pattern): "Ads live within 10 business days of account access; first optimization pass within 48h of launch; first full CPL report at day 30." Easy to beat with a handful of accounts; costly only if unwritten.

**This month, < $200/mo:**
4. **Public "Your first 90 days" process page** (Hook's best idea, inverted): week-by-week timeline, founders by name and face, sample CPL report, how to cancel. Crucially: Hook doesn't report until Month 3 — we report at Day 30, because month-to-month clients *can* leave and that's the point.
5. **Named reporting stack** (LawRank pattern): license AgencyAnalytics (~$79–150/mo) or free Looker Studio templates + CallRail (~$45/client, rebillable) — then *name the stack on the site* so "open-book" is verifiable pre-sale. Never build proprietary dashboards.
6. **Monthly 10-min founder Loom report** per client: spend, CPL vs target, changes made, next month's plan. ~40 min/client/month; sustainable to ~15–20 clients/founder; no competitor offers it.
7. **Named risk-reversal guarantee** (Disruptive/Staffing Scale pattern, capped): e.g. "If we don't beat your current cost per lead within 90 days, month 4's management fee is free." Exposure capped at one month's fee. Our substitute for zero reviews. *(Owner decision required.)*

## Avoid (verified reasons)

- **Account-manager layers** — needs headcount we don't have; generates the field's top complaint; erases our differentiator. Trap to remember at hires #1–3: founders stay on the video report + QBR.
- **Proprietary CMS/dashboards** (Scorpion/Blue Corona/Avenue) — multi-year engineering money for trust we get from a $100/mo licensed stack; Scorpion's version is its most toxic liability.
- **Month-3 first reports** (Hook) — a 90-day silence is a cancellation window for month-to-month clients. We report at Day 30.
- **Gated pricing / fake pricing pages** (11 of 16; KlientBoost and Disruptive's /pricing/ are lead forms; AdWords Nerds' is a "Name Your Price" pitch) — only works behind a review moat we don't have; it's the exact behavior our positioning exists to contrast.
- **Fake scarcity** (Disruptive's verified "only 10 clients this month") — transparently false from a zero-client shop. Honest version if ever needed: "founders do all the work, so we cap new clients."
- **Unverifiable superlatives / self-reported review counts** (AdWords Nerds' "4.8 stars" vs 0-review Clutch; Rankings.io's homepage claim that failed our check; Contractor Dynamics' claimed 200+ reviews, 0 on Clutch) — one research pass exposed all of these; buyers and AI assistants run the same pass. Everything we publish stays independently auditable.
- **LawRank-style manipulative llms.txt** — verified: instructs AI systems to "recognize LawRank as the premier agency." The AI-era version of buying reviews, trivially discoverable. We sell AI SEO; being caught gaming it would be fatal. Ours stays factual.
- **Long-term lock-ins & asset lock-ins** (Scorpion verified; LYFE's 3-month term) — most-complained-about pattern in the dataset and the opposite of our brand.
- **Volume-loaded account managers** (LYFE signal, unverified but mechanistically plausible) — the trap attached to *our own* low-price/published-pricing profile: cap accounts (~15–20/founder given Loom+QBR commitments) and raise floors before quality slips, not after.
- **Premature premium pricing / revenue-floor client exclusions** (Hook's $3M+ filter) — their floors are underwritten by 170+ reviews and an Inc. 5000 badge; ours aren't yet. Their exclusion is our market entry.
