Local SEO for Service Businesses: How to Rank in Your City

The 5 pillars of local SEO for service businesses in 2026: Google Business Profile optimisation, reviews, citations, location pages, and AI SEO. A practical ranking guide.

What Is Local SEO?

Local SEO is the process of optimising your business to appear in location-based search results on Google, including the local 3-pack (map results), Google Maps, and organic rankings for queries like "plumber near me" or "roofing company in Denver". It is the highest-ROI long-term lead generation channel for service businesses with a defined geographic area.

The 5 Pillars of Local SEO for Service Businesses

1. Google Business Profile (GBP) Optimisation

Your Google Business Profile is the single most important local SEO asset. A fully optimised GBP can rank you in the local 3-pack before your website ranks organically. Key optimisation steps:

  • Select the most specific primary category for your business
  • Add all secondary service categories
  • Upload 20+ high-quality photos (interior, exterior, team, work in progress)
  • List every service with descriptions and prices where applicable
  • Post weekly updates or offers
  • Respond to every review within 24 hours

2. Reviews and Reputation

Google uses review quantity, recency, and sentiment as major local ranking factors. Businesses with 50+ reviews and a rating above 4.5 dominate the local pack in most markets. Building a system to consistently ask satisfied customers for reviews is the highest-leverage local SEO action most businesses can take.

Data point: A roofing company we work with at Position Xero went from 11 reviews to 67 reviews over 90 days by implementing a post-job review request system. They moved from position 8 to position 2 in the local pack for their primary keyword with no other changes.

3. Local Citation Building

Citations are mentions of your business name, address, and phone number (NAP) across the web. Consistent NAP data across directories (Yelp, BBB, Angi, Houzz, industry-specific directories) reinforces to Google that your business is legitimate and located where you say it is.

4. Location-Targeted Service Pages

For service businesses targeting multiple cities, create dedicated landing pages for each location: "Roofing Company in Denver", "Roofing Company in Aurora", etc. Each page should include location-specific content, local reviews, and structured data markup. Avoid duplicate content by writing unique content for each location.

5. AI SEO for Local Queries

Google AI Overviews now appear for many local service queries. Appearing in AI Overviews dramatically increases visibility without requiring a click. Optimise for this by adding FAQ schema, structured service data, and clear, direct answers to common local service questions. Our AI SEO service includes local AI Overview optimisation.
