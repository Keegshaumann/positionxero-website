## Phase 0 — Register the US LLC (unblocks half this document)

- ☐ Form a Wyoming (or Delaware) LLC via **Doola** (doola.com) or Firstbase — built for non-US founders, a few hundred dollars, done in days. Gets you: a real legal entity (personal-liability shield), an EIN, a US business bank account (Mercury/Relay), and **a registered-agent address you are legally entitled to use** on directories and legal documents.
- Do this **at or before the first client**, not after the second — it's what makes taking US client money clean, and "Position Xero LLC, a US-registered company" becomes *true*.
- After registration: update terms.html (entity name + Wyoming governing law becomes natural), and directories that need a legal/mailing address can use the registered-agent address **where the platform's rules allow it** (mailing/legal address ≠ a physical office claim — never present it as a place of business, and it still doesn't qualify for GBP).
