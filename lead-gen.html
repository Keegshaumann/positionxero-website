Our Channels

FAQ

Lead Generation Questions

Lead generation is the process of attracting and converting strangers into prospects who have expressed interest in your product or service. It involves identifying your target audience, reaching them through the right channels (ads, SEO, cold outreach), and capturing their details through landing pages, forms, or direct contact so your sales team can follow up and close.

We qualify leads through targeting criteria (ensuring only the right audience sees your campaigns), pre-qualification questions on landing pages, and initial follow-up sequences. A qualified lead meets your criteria for Budget, Authority, Need, and Timeline, the BANT framework. Only prospects who pass these criteria are passed to your sales team.

Our starter campaigns typically generate 20 to 50 qualified leads per month, depending on your industry, ad spend, and target market. We set realistic expectations during the initial audit call based on your specific niche and budget, not inflated promises.

A lead is anyone who has submitted their details. A qualified lead is someone who has confirmed they have the budget, decision-making authority, genuine need, and timeline to buy your service. We build qualification into the front of our funnels so you spend your time selling to people who can actually become clients.
