Careers at Position Xero

We don't just run ads. We build revenue machines.

Position Xero is founder-led. Ronnie James Botes and Keegan Shane Haumann founded it and still run it. We're hiring for three roles, and we hire for all three on an ongoing basis. Ronnie and Keegan are reachable directly, not through a chain of messages. All three roles are fully remote and open to applicants anywhere in the world.

The Company

Founder-Led, and Still Hands-On.

Position Xero is a performance marketing and lead generation agency, founded and still led by Ronnie James Botes and Keegan Shane Haumann. Ronnie’s background is web development and SEO. Keegan’s is full-stack development: the sites, landing pages and tracking that campaigns run on. Most ads management is handled by the team, and some of the development work is done by other developers. Both founders are still in the work, and the roles below are additions to work that is already running. You can read about both founders here.

We are run from South Africa and every engagement is delivered remotely, to clients in the United States and South Africa. Campaign builds, reporting calls, ad approvals and SEO work all happen inside ad accounts, analytics and websites, not in a boardroom. These roles are remote because that is already how the agency operates.

The work itself is Google Ads and Meta Ads, lead generation, SEO including AI and LLM search optimisation, and conversion-focused web design. The founders also run Cognexa, an AI company, and what gets learned there feeds the AI search work here.

Every client is month-to-month with no lock-in, and reporting is open-book on cost per qualified lead. If you want to see how a client account actually runs, week by week, here is the process in full.

Why Here

What You Actually Get

No perks list, because there isn't one to invent. Here is what is genuinely true about working on this team.

The Founders Are Reachable

Position Xero is founder-led. Ronnie and Keegan are reachable directly, not through a chain of messages, and the people who make the decisions are people you can talk to.

Real Ownership From Day One

You own the account, the build or the call outright. Not a slice of somebody else's work, and not a queue of tickets someone else scoped.

The Numbers Are Open

Clients get open-book cost-per-lead reporting and a live Looker Studio dashboard. Internally it works the same way, so you can see what a campaign cost and what it produced, and judge your own work honestly.

Month-to-Month Keeps Everyone Honest

Every client can cancel at the end of any billing month. Nobody here is protected by a twelve-month contract, which means the work has to be good. That is the standard, stated plainly.

Two Markets, Not One

You work across United States and South African accounts. Different buyer behaviour, different currencies, different search intent, often in the same week.

AI Work Is Not a Side Quest

AI search optimisation ships with every engagement, built on what the founders learn running Cognexa. There is scope to work on it rather than read about it.

The Process

How We Hire

Four steps. No unpaid spec work, and no eight-round interview loop.

01

You Apply

Fill in the form on the role page. It comes straight to us, with no recruiter and no screening software in between.

02

A Person Reads It

Every application is read by a person here, not by screening software. Specifics beat adjectives, so tell us what you actually did and how you knew it worked.

03

A Short Intro Call

A video call to go through what you have done, what we are building, and whether the working hours genuinely fit on both sides. A founder joins where they can.

04

A Practical Session

A short, real piece of work talked through together (an account walkthrough, a mock call, or a small build) rather than a whiteboard quiz. Then a decision either way.

Straight Talk

Before You Apply

Three things worth knowing before you spend time on an application.

You own real work, and you answer for it. Position Xero is founder-led, and the roles here are defined by what you own rather than where you sit on a ladder. If you want a defined ladder and a job description that never moves, this is not it. If you want to own an account, a build or a number outright, it is.

Remote worldwide, with one real constraint. Work from anywhere. Client work runs across United States and South African business hours, so meaningful overlap with those working days matters more than which country you are in.

Pay is discussed on the first call. Competitive and role-dependent. We would rather agree the number in a conversation than publish a range that fits nobody. The Sales Closer package is commission-based with uncapped earnings, which is stated up front so nobody wastes a call.
