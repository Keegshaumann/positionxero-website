The Role

Commission Only. You Build Your Own Pipeline.

Position Xero is a founder-led performance marketing agency, founded and still led by Ronnie James Botes and Keegan Shane Haumann. We run Google Ads, Meta Ads, lead generation, SEO and conversion-focused web design. The agency is run from South Africa and every engagement is delivered remotely to businesses in the United States and South Africa. We are adding to the closing side of the business on an ongoing basis.

Start with the part most job ads bury. This role is commission-based with uncapped earnings and no base. That is a strength if you close well and a dealbreaker if you need a guaranteed number every month. We would rather you decide that now than three calls in. The structure is walked through openly on the first call.

Now the part that decides whether you should apply at all. This is a cold outreach and closing role. You build your own pipeline: cold calling, and cold outreach over LinkedIn and DM. Nobody hands you a booked diary. If that sentence just cost us your application, it did its job. The wrong hire here is someone who can only close what somebody else booked.

Warm leads do exist, and they are earned rather than issued. The free audit funnel on this site takes a prospect through questions about their business and, where SEO is the focus, hands them a graded report card on their own website. Those people have raised their hand and seen something specific and unflattering about their own setup, so they are the best conversations we have. They get allocated to closers who have already proved they can close. Prove it on the pipeline you built yourself and that allocation follows. Treat it as upside, not as the starting position.

The selling style is diagnosis first, close second. Our about page says publicly that we don't take on clients we can't help, and that we'd rather refer someone to the right specialist than take a retainer and deliver mediocre results. That is not a line you get to quietly ignore once you're on a call. Diagnosis-first is also the only approach that works commercially here: everything is month-to-month with no lock-in, so anyone who gets oversold cancels at the end of the billing month and the commission leaves with them.

So the standard is simple. We are growing the closing side of the business, and we are measuring this role on what stays. Good here looks like fewer, better-qualified clients who are still with us in month six, not a bigger number in month one and a churn problem in month four.

The Work

What You'll Actually Do

Concrete work, not a list of verbs. This is what a week in the role is made of.

Build Your Own Pipeline From Cold

Cold calling, and cold outreach over LinkedIn and DM. You find the businesses, open the conversation and book the call yourself. Warm inbound from the free audit funnel gets allocated to closers who have already proved they can close, so you earn it rather than start with it.

Diagnose Before You Pitch

Ask what they spend now, what a lead currently costs them, what their close rate is, and how much extra work they can genuinely take on. A contractor who can't service forty more jobs a month does not need forty more leads, and saying so early is the job. Where the call did come out of the audit funnel, you open with what that report actually found, not with a pitch deck.

Match the Prospect to the Right Service

Google Ads or Meta Ads, lead generation, SEO and AI SEO, web design, or a combination. Our pricing is published at pricing.md, so you sell against a number the prospect can already see rather than one invented on the call.

Close Month-to-Month Retainers

No annual contracts, no lock-in, no cancellation fee. You close on the strength of the plan and the diagnosis, not on a contract that traps someone for twelve months after they've stopped being happy.

Say No Out Loud

When someone isn't a fit (wrong budget, wrong stage, wrong problem), tell them plainly and point them somewhere better. Turning down revenue is part of this job, not a failure of it. The FAQ already answers most of the pricing and contract questions you'll be asked.

Run Your Own CRM and Follow-Up

Log every call, keep the pipeline current, and run your own written follow-up. This is discipline you bring with you rather than something you get chased for, and on a self-sourced pipeline the follow-up is where most of the money is.

Must-Haves

What We're Looking For

Every item here is something we can check in a 30-minute call or a short practical session. If it can't be checked, it's on the nice-to-have list instead.

  • You can self-source. Cold calling, and cold outreach over LinkedIn and DM, run by you. If every deal you have closed arrived in a diary somebody else filled, this will be a hard landing.
  • You have personally closed B2B service or retainer deals on video calls. Sourcing without closing is not enough either: pure appointment setting is a different job.
  • You sell consultatively and can show it: you ask about current cost per lead, close rate and capacity before you talk about price.
  • Clear, plain-spoken English, written and spoken. You'll be on calls with owner-operators in both markets (roofing, HVAC and plumbing contractors, attorneys, agency owners) and following up in writing afterwards.
  • You're comfortable being paid on commission with uncapped earnings and no base.
  • You can tell a prospect they aren't a fit and walk away from the revenue.
  • CRM and follow-up discipline you run yourself, without being chased for it.
  • You can hold your own activity standard on a week where nothing lands. Cold pipelines are lumpy and this role is paid on outcomes.
  • Meaningful overlap with United States and South African business hours. Where you live is up to you; when you're reachable is not.
Bonus Points

Nice to Have

None of these are dealbreakers. Apply without them.

  • You've sold marketing, SEO or advertising services specifically.
  • You understand what cost per lead, cost per acquisition and return on ad spend mean well enough to talk credibly about them without overpromising.
  • You've sold month-to-month rather than annual contracts before.
  • Exposure to the United States market, the South African market, or both.
  • Experience selling to home-services or legal businesses.
The Details

How This Role Works

The terms, stated plainly, before you spend time on an application.

Where the pipeline comes from. You build it. Cold calling, and cold outreach over LinkedIn and DM. Warm inbound from the free audit funnel is allocated to closers who have proved they can close, so plan on it as upside rather than as your starting position.

Fully remote, anywhere in the world. This role is 100% remote. Position Xero is run from South Africa and all client work is already delivered remotely. That is how the agency operates, not a concession made for this role. We are open to applicants anywhere in the world.

Hours. Client work runs across United States and South African business days. We need meaningful overlap with those hours; the exact schedule is agreed with you.

Employment type. Commission-based contract with uncapped earnings.

Pay. Commission-based with uncapped earnings and no base. The structure is walked through on the first call. We're not publishing a figure here because the honest version of this conversation happens live, with the pipeline and the retainer sizes in front of us both on the call.

Who you work with. Position Xero is founder-led. Ronnie James Botes (web development and SEO) and Keegan Shane Haumann (full-stack developer) built the agency, still run it, and are reachable directly rather than through a chain of messages.

The hiring process. You apply through the form on this page, a person here reads it, and if it looks like a fit we set up a call. The steps are laid out on the careers page.
