Full Stack Developer (Remote)

You build the pages the ad spend lands on.

Fully remote, open worldwide · Full-time · Founder-led, with the founders reachable directly · Overlap with US and South African business hours

Apply for This Role →

The Role

The Job, Described Honestly

Position Xero is a founder-led performance marketing agency, founded and still led by Ronnie James Botes (web development and SEO) and Keegan Shane Haumann (full-stack developer). We are run from South Africa and every engagement is delivered remotely to clients in the United States and South Africa. Every client is month-to-month and can leave at the end of any billing month, so the work has to hold up. That is the standard, not a slogan.

This role exists because the pages are the product. Paid traffic lands somewhere, and that somewhere decides whether a click becomes a booked job. A broken conversion event or a 200ms regression is a measurable cost, not a ticket in a backlog. Build work is already running here, and some of it is done by other developers on the team. This role is an addition to it: a set of pages and tracking that you own end to end. We hire for this role on an ongoing basis.

The site you are reading is the reference build. Hand-written semantic HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No build step, no framework, no bundler. Root-relative extensionless URLs, JSON-LD on every indexable page, and Core Web Vitals treated as a requirement rather than a report. View source before you apply. It is the fastest way to know whether this is your kind of work. The same standards run through our web design work for clients.

Fundamentals outrank framework trivia here. We are not going to ask you to recite hooks from memory. We will ask what made a page slow and what you did about it.

The Work

What You'll Actually Do

Six things, and they are the whole job. No hidden seventh column of admin.

Build the Pages Campaigns Run On

Landing pages and websites in hand-written semantic HTML and CSS, with vanilla JavaScript where JavaScript earns its place. Campaigns go live within 10 business days of account access, so pages ship inside that window.

Implement the Tracking

GA4 events, Google Ads conversion actions, the Meta pixel and Conversions API, form endpoints, and CallRail on phone-heavy campaigns. If the conversion does not fire, the cost-per-lead number is fiction.

Own Structured Data and the Plumbing

JSON-LD that validates, canonicals, redirects, sitemaps and 404 handling. Also the judgement to leave a claim out of the markup when the page cannot support it.

Keep Core Web Vitals Honest

LCP, CLS and INP measured on live pages, not on a staging build. Fonts, images and render-blocking CSS are the usual suspects and they are yours to police.

Make Every Form Accessible

Labels, focus states, keyboard navigation, landmarks and colour contrast. Every form on these sites is a lead-capture form, and an inaccessible form is a lost lead.

App and Automation Work

The founders also run Cognexa, an AI company, and there is scope to work on builds and automations there. Scope, not a promise of a specific project.

Must-Haves

What We're Looking For

Everything here is something we could check in a thirty-minute call or a short practical session.

  • Strong HTML, CSS and JavaScript fundamentals: semantic markup, layout without a framework, DOM work without jQuery. Frameworks are welcome; framework-dependence is not.
  • Performance as a habit. You understand LCP, CLS and INP, know why fonts, images and render-blocking CSS matter, and have actually fixed a slow page rather than only measured one.
  • Accessibility basics that hold up under a keyboard: labels, focus states, landmarks and colour contrast.
  • Structured data in practice: JSON-LD you have written that validates, and the judgement to leave out a claim a page cannot support.
  • Comfort with APIs, modern tooling, and tracking implementation: GA4 events, conversion tags, form endpoints.
  • Something public you can point at: a repository, a live site, or a page you built. This role is unusually easy to evidence.
  • Meaningful overlap with United States and South African business hours. Where you live is up to you; when you are reachable is not.
  • Clear written and spoken English. You will explain technical decisions to business owners in both markets.

No degree is required. We care more about work you can show us and talk through than about a year count.

Nice To Have

Useful, Not Required

None of these are dealbreakers. Apply without them.

  • React, Next.js or a similar framework for app work.
  • Node, Python, Cloudflare Workers or serverless functions.
  • Landing-page conversion rate optimisation experience.
  • Working with AI APIs or automation.
  • Enough design sense to build from a rough direction rather than a finished design file.
The Terms

How This Role Works

The things applicants ask on the first call, answered before it.

Fully remote, anywhere in the world. This role is 100% remote. We are run from South Africa and all client work is delivered remotely. That is how the agency already operates, not a concession made for this role.

Hours. Client work runs across United States and South African business days. We need meaningful overlap with those hours; the exact schedule is agreed with you.

Employment type. Full-time.

Pay. Competitive and discussed on the first call. We would rather agree the number in a conversation than publish a range that fits nobody.

Who you work with. Position Xero is founder-led. Ronnie James Botes (web development and SEO) and Keegan Shane Haumann (full-stack developer) are reachable directly, not through a chain of messages. Read about them here.

The hiring process. You apply through the form on this page, a person here reads it, and if it looks like a fit we set up a call. The steps are laid out on the careers page.
